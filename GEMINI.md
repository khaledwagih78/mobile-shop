# Antigravity Design Rules — No AI Slop

When designing, updating, or reviewing the UI for this ERP application:

1. **Brand & Identity Consistency**:
   - The primary brand color is `--primary: #0f4c5c` (Petrol Teal).
   - The surface color is `#ffffff`, background is `#f2f5f6`, text is `#15252b`.
   - Never use generic AI purple gradients (`#8B5CF6`), generic neon glows, or random dark voids.

2. **No AI Tells**:
   - No emojis used as icons (no 📊, 🤖, 👑, 🛒 in buttons or cards).
   - No generic centered single-column templates.
   - High contrast text (WCAG AA): dark text on light backgrounds (`#15252b`), never washed-out gray (`gray-400`).
   - Clean tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) on all currency, prices, quantities, and accounting data.
   - Unified border-radius (`10px`) across all cards, tables, inputs, and buttons.
   - No fake touch numpads or gimmicks.
